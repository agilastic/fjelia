Fjelia unifies your workouts, sleep, and recovery into one adaptive training hub. Plan your season, prevent overtraining, and ask your AI coach what to do next.
Core features are free. Coaching is powered by flexible AI—including an optional on-device model for complete privacy.
KEY FEATURES
• Adaptive Multi-Sport Plans: Build dynamic plans for running, cycling, or multisport that automatically adjust based on your fatigue and completed workouts.
• Recovery & HRV Insights: Track resting heart rate, sleep trends, and HRV to know exactly when to push and when to rest.
• Unified Health Data: Sync seamlessly with Apple Health to track load, heart-rate zones, body composition, and performance trends in one view.
• Privacy-First AI: Run coaching models locally on compatible devices. Your data stays on your phone.
