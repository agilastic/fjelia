VERSION 1.3.2: WHAT IS NEW FOR REVIEW
New: FjeliaPro, an optional auto-renewable subscription (group "FjeliaPro": fjelia.pro.monthly, fjelia.pro.yearly), submitted with this version. Earlier features stay free.

1. WHERE TO FIND THE SUBSCRIPTION
- Settings (menu button, top left) > FjeliaPro.
- Also: Plan tab > "Pro analyses" card, and Load tab > training stats card.
The sheet shows the benefits, each plan's price and period from the App Store, "Get FjeliaPro", "Restore purchases", "Manage subscription" (once subscribed), the auto-renewal terms and links to the Terms of Use (Apple's standard EULA) and privacy policy. The EULA link is also in every localization's description.
Sandbox purchases work: Fjelia's server checks the transaction with the App Store Server API (production, then sandbox).

2. WHAT FJELIAPRO UNLOCKS
In-app digital features only: a Pro AI model with its own budget, plan check, week review, race plan, season analysis, training stats, Memory Cards, and an ECG analysis view (signal findings, not a diagnosis).

3. ACCOUNTS AND DATA
No Fjelia accounts (no sign-up, no login), so no account deletion (5.1.1(v)); the subscription is tied to the Apple ID. Data stays on the device; Settings > System > Data Export offers export, backup and restore. No user-generated content is shared.

4. SETUP FOR REVIEW
- Launch, tap "Get Started", connect Apple Health or skip, set a race goal or skip.
- Tabs: Health (readiness), Plan (adaptive plan and calendar), Load (fitness, fatigue, form), Coach (AI coach).
- Attachment fjelia_review_1.3.2.zip holds a screen recording (fjelia_review_1.3.2.mp4: where FjeliaPro is offered, its sheet with prices and links) and sample data (fjelia_review_sample_1.3.2.zip, five months up to 17 Nov 2026 and a plan). Unzip it once, save the sample ZIP to Files unopened, then Settings > System > Data Export > "Restore from backup".
- Vendor accounts (Polar, Garmin, Suunto, COROS, Hilo/Aktiia, Renpho, MyWhoosh) are optional; the app works with Apple Health or manual entries alone.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES AND PRIVACY
- HealthKit: read for analysis on the device; with permission, workouts, sleep, heart rate, weight and steps from connected devices are written. Never used for advertising or sold.
- Cloud AI is optional and named before use. Free AI: Fjelia's service ai.fjelia.com (open-weight Qwen), with a random installation ID against abuse. Own key: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral or DeepSeek, sent directly. FjeliaPro: Fjelia's service sends Pro requests to Mistral (EU), OpenAI as fallback, without name, e-mail or GPS tracks.
- Consent: chat and Pro analyses are sent only after confirmation (every message by default, or once per session). With Pro, the morning proposal ("Coach writes the morning push", Settings > Training) sends the day's plan, weather and a health summary without asking each time, but only if allowed: it is off until the user agrees in a one-time question after the subscription starts; switchable any time.
- Subscription check: only the transaction ID goes to push.fjelia.com; Apple handles payment.
- On-device AI: Apple Intelligence, or a model downloaded once from huggingface.co.
- Vendor accounts: users sign in with their own account; Fjelia is not affiliated with these vendors.
- Weather, maps: Open-Meteo, Bright Sky (DWD), Pirate Weather, WeatherAPI.com, RainViewer, OpenStreetMap.
- Bug reports: only when sent by the user; diagnostics only if enabled.
No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. Logins and keys: iOS Keychain.
Privacy policy: https://www.fjelia.com/privacy.html

6. REGIONAL AVAILABILITY
Same worldwide, 13 app languages. Bright Sky weather in Germany, Open-Meteo elsewhere; Apple Intelligence per Apple's availability.

7. REGULATED CONTENT
Fitness and wellness app, not a medical device; no diagnosis, treatment or medical advice. BP and ECG data come from the user's devices or Apple Health. The coach is told not to diagnose and to advise seeing a doctor.
