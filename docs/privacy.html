Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Your health database stays on your device. No Fjelia account is required. You choose connected services and AI options. Data you approve for cloud AI, reports you send to support, and requests to weather or map providers are handled as described below.

1. Who is responsible

Fjelia is a free, open-source hobby project by Alex Ebeling-Hoppe. Questions about privacy: use the contact form.

2. What data Fjelia handles

  • Health and fitness data from the devices and services you connect and from Apple Health: activities, heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, steps, blood oxygen, energy, blood pressure, weight and body composition.
  • Things you enter: a profile (for example sex, birth year, height, training preferences), race goals, manual measurements, notes and settings.
  • Location, only while the app is in use and only if you allow it, to find the weather for heat-acclimation and planned workouts. You can use a fixed location instead or switch the feature off.
  • AI chat messages that you send to the coach.

3. Where your data is stored

Your health database is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. Passwords, tokens and personal API keys use the system keychain. Widgets read a small summary on the same device. Backups and exports are created when you choose. The developer cannot access this local database. Cloud AI requests and support reports are handled separately, as described below.

4. Services you connect

Your device connects directly to each service to download your data. For Garmin, it can also send planned workouts to your calendar. Sign-in details or tokens are stored on your device. Each service handles your data according to its own privacy policy.

Polar Flow login (optional). Polar's official interface only shares the last 30 days. If you enter your Polar Flow e-mail and password in the Polar settings, Fjelia signs in to Polar's website once during the first history import or a full sync, reads your own training diary and imports older sessions. The login is kept in the system keychain on your device, is sent only to Polar and can be removed at any time in the same place.

Bluetooth devices (Hilo, Renpho scales, Garmin watches) connect directly to your phone. Fjelia asks for Bluetooth permission for this.

5. Weather, radar and maps

To show weather and heat information, Fjelia sends coordinates to Open-Meteo and Bright Sky (Germany). Builds with the optional keys can also use Pirate Weather and WeatherAPI. Radar images come from RainViewer and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. These servers can see your IP address and the map area you view. They are contacted when you use these features.

6. The AI coach

  • Free AI: the included cloud service at ai.fjelia.com needs no personal API key or separate AI account. After your confirmation, your message and a health/training summary are sent to this service for processing.
  • Apple Intelligence: runs on compatible devices with Apple Intelligence enabled and a supported language. Fjelia does not send these requests to a cloud AI provider.
  • Your own cloud provider: optionally configure OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral or DeepSeek with your personal API key. Approved requests go directly to that provider and are subject to its privacy policy.
  • Local Qwen: optional models are downloaded from their model host after you choose a download. On supported hardware, inference then runs on your device.

To keep Free AI reliably available for everyone, requests include a random installation ID. It helps identify unusual use and block abusive requests. The ID contains neither your name nor account details. The service can process technical connection and request-usage data to operate and protect the service.

Cloud chat asks for confirmation once per session by default. You can choose confirmation before every message in Settings. Cloud briefings ask for confirmation on first use and after a change of AI provider or model. The context summary leaves out names and GPS tracks; anything you type in a message is sent with that message. Notes you add to an activity (for example why it was shorter) are part of the summary.

6a. FjeliaPro subscription

FjeliaPro is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple. Apple handles payment; Fjelia never sees your payment details. To unlock Pro, the app sends the purchase's transaction ID to push.fjelia.com. The service asks Apple's App Store Server API for the subscription status and keeps the transaction ID, product, expiry date, status and a generated access key for Fjelia AI Pro. Apple also notifies this service of renewals, cancellations and refunds. You manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple account settings.

With Pro, Fjelia AI answers with the Pro models on your subscription's own access key, without the installation ID: ProAI for the chat and the daily proposals, the larger ProAI-deep for the race plan and the season analysis. After your confirmation, chat messages and the health and training summary are sent as described in section 6. The Pro analyses (plan check, week review, race plan, season) also send your plan by weeks, the last days as they went, race goals without their names and your sport statistics. Pro reports and your Memory Cards are stored on your device; Memory Cards are sent with chat messages so the coach can use them.

Morning proposal: when the readiness check suggests changing the day's training and "Coach writes the morning push" is on (Settings → Training, on by default with Pro), Fjelia sends that day's and the next day's sessions, the rest of the week, your training settings, the weather forecast, race goals without names and the health summary to Fjelia AI Pro without asking each time. Turn the switch off to keep these proposals on your device; they are then made by fixed rules.

Fjelia AI Pro sends requests to Mistral models through an EU-based server by default. If Mistral is unavailable, the service may use OpenAI as a fallback. Neither the service nor the AI providers receive your name, e-mail address or GPS tracks from Fjelia. Without an active subscription no Pro request is sent.

7. What Fjelia does not do

  • No advertising libraries, marketing trackers or automatic crash-reporting libraries. Free AI request-usage data is described in section 6.
  • No Fjelia account and no server storing your health database.
  • No selling of your data. The developer cannot access your local health database. Approved Free AI requests and support reports are processed as described above.

7a. TestFlight

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple manages the installation. Apple may collect tester information, including your Apple ID, crash reports and feedback you submit, as described in its privacy policy. The developer only sees the information Apple provides in App Store Connect, such as installation counts and submitted feedback. Your health and training database is not included.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting the app removes its local database. You can disconnect services in Fjelia and revoke access in each service’s settings or in Apple Health. This does not delete data held by other services, such as Garmin Connect. Manage that data directly with those services.

9. Your rights

You manage your local health database on your device. To access, correct or delete a support report you sent, use the contact form. For data held by a connected service or AI provider, contact that provider. You can also use the form above to ask about this policy.

10. Children

Fjelia is not aimed at children.

11. This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). It uses no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Your language choice is saved locally in your browser. GitHub may process technical logs, including your IP address. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contacting support

If you send a website message or an in-app bug report, your email address, subject and message are sent to www.fjelia.com. They are used to handle your request and are separate from your local health database. Diagnostics are excluded by default. They are only sent if you enable them. You can review the log first, as it may contain account or health details. Please do not include passwords, API keys or personal health readings. You can also send feedback through TestFlight or post an issue publicly on GitHub.

12. Changes

This page and its date are updated when Fjelia’s data handling changes. You can view the full history in the source repository.
